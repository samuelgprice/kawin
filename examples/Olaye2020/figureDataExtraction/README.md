# Digitised figure data (Olaye & Ojo 2020)

These CSV files hold curves and experimental points digitised with
WebPlotDigitizer from Figures 5 and 6 of Olaye and Ojo (2020),
"Leapfrog/Dufort-Frankel explicit scheme for diffusion-controlled moving
interphase boundary problems". Each row is an `x, y` pair in the axis units of
the source figure.

They are used by the Olaye 2020 replication scripts in `examples/Olaye2020/`
and by plotting tests in `kawin/tests/test_diffusion.py`.

Only the CSVs are tracked. The WebPlotDigitizer projects (`*.tar`) and the
figure images stay local, because the images are copies of the published
figures.
